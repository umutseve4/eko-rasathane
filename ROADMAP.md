- Assumption radar and model comparison
- Shareable, privacy-safe Model Cards

- 15–25 BUÜ Ekonometri students
- Accessibility and performance audit
- Copyright/KVKK review for concrete upload and sharing flows
- Success gate: ≥60% create first Model Card, median <20 minutes
